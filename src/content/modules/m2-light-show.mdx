Loops aren't just for moving. Anything the robot *does* can repeat — including its light.

A blink is really four steps: **on → wait → off → wait**. Put those in a loop and you've built a rhythm machine.

**Try this after:** make it blink *fast* three times then *slow* three times. (Hint: two loops, different wait times.) That pattern — short-short-long — is how real machines signal errors to humans. You've seen it on routers your whole life.

**Optional side-quest 🟢 easy:** design a "heartbeat" — two quick blinks, long pause, repeat.
