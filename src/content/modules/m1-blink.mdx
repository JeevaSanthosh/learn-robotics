Time to write your first program. Drag blocks from the tray into the workspace, snap them together top-to-bottom, and hit **Run**. The robot runs your blocks in order, like a recipe.

One shortcut worth knowing: a <Term id="loop">loop</Term> repeats the same steps so you don't have to drag them three times. Try the `repeat` block!
