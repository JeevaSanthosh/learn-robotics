A <Term id="robot">robot</Term> is any machine that does three things in a loop: it **senses**, it **decides**, and it **acts**.

Your simulated robot down there 👇 has all three:

- A <Term id="sensor">sensor</Term> on its nose that measures distance to walls.
- A tiny computer brain that runs *your* program.
- Two wheels and an <Term id="led">LED</Term> — its <Term id="actuator">actuators</Term>, the parts that actually *do* things.

That's the whole secret of robotics. Everything else in this course is just getting better at those three steps.

**Next lesson:** you make the LED blink. It's the "hello!" of every robot ever built.
