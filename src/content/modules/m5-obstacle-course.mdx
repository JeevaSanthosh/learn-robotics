This is the one. A walled course, a target pad, and every block you've earned.

The rule that makes it a *robotics* mission: **your program must use the distance sensor**. You can hard-code a path — but move one wall and hard-coded paths die. Sensor-driven robots adapt. Sense → decide → act, in a loop: the definition you met in lesson one, now built by your own hands.

A strategy that works (there are others — find yours):

1. Repeat until you're on the pad-ish side: **if** space ahead → move; **else** → turn.
2. Blink the LED when you arrive, because victory laps matter.
