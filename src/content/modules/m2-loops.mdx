Look at the arena below: the path to the target zig-zags — forward, turn, forward, turn… the *same two moves over and over*.

You could drag eight blocks. Or you could teach the robot the **pattern** once and say "do that 4 times." That's a <Term id="loop">loop</Term>, and it's the single most-used idea in all of programming.
