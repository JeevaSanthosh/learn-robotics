Until now, your robot followed orders blindly. Time to give it senses.

The green **distance to wall** block is a <Term id="sensor">sensor</Term> — it reports a *number*: how many squares of empty space are ahead. Numbers alone do nothing; the magic is pairing them with a <Term id="conditional">conditional</Term> — an **if** block: *if the distance is bigger than 2 → move.*
